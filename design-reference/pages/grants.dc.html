How grants are selected

The Grants Selection committee oversees grantmaking and manages a team of volunteer reviewers. Proposals are scored against a shared rubric so selection stays transparent and equitable, then shortlisted projects go to an eligible-member vote.

Volunteer as a reviewer

Scoring rubric

Weightings to be set by the Grants Selection committee

Community-identified needThe project responds to a priority named by the community
Organizational strengthLeadership, track record and financial health
Impact & reachClear, measurable benefit for the people served
SustainabilityA plan for results to last beyond the grant
Alignment with our valuesAuthenticity, equity, legacy and representation

Selection process

How a proposal becomes a grant

  1. 1Nominate

    Members suggest partners and proposals.

  2. 2Review

    The Grants Selection committee scores each proposal.

  3. 3Publish

    An admin publishes proposals for members to read.

  4. 4Vote

    Voting members decide; quorum and majority rules apply.

The five-criterion rubric

Community needOrganizational capacityImpactSustainabilityAlignment

Weightings are set by the Grants Selection committee.
