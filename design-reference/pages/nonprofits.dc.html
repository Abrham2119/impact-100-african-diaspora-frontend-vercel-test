For organizations

Grantmaking through USAFI’s partner network.

Grants go to high-impact organizations in Sub-Saharan Africa within USAFI’s partner network. If your organization isn’t a USAFI partner yet, start by expressing interest — members may also nominate organizations they know well.

How it works

  1. 1Join the USAFI partner network

    Grants go to USAFI partner organizations. Express interest and USAFI will guide you through partnership.

  2. 2Nomination & proposal summary

    Members may nominate partners they know. Chapter leaders summarize each project, its budget, and any conflicts of interest.

  3. 3Volunteer review

    The Grants Selection committee’s volunteer reviewers score proposals against a transparent rubric.

  4. 4Due diligence

    USAFI Impact, as fiscal sponsor, confirms standing and charitable use of funds.

  5. 5Member vote

    Eligible members vote on preferred projects. Approved grants are paid by USAFI Impact.

Quick eligibility check

Draft criteria — final guidelines coming soon

1. Is your organization based and working in Sub-Saharan Africa?
2. Are you a USAFI partner organization, or willing to become one?
3. Is your organization led by people from the community it serves?
4. Can you share a budget and basic financial records for due diligence?
