Our model

Pool our gifts. Decide together.

Members give in one campaign cycle, pool their money into grants, and vote on where it goes. Levels, thresholds and dates are charter settings — they come from one source and appear the same on every page.

1

Give in the cycle

The campaign cycle runs October 1 – March 31. Pledges, payments and eligibility are tracked per cycle.

2

Review together

Volunteer committees review proposals and score them on five criteria — community need, organizational capacity, impact, sustainability and alignment.

3

Vote and fund

Voting members decide. One member, one vote per ballot, whatever the member’s level. Grants go to partners in USAFI hubs across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Membership starts at $250

You become a member once at least $250 has been received in the current cycle.

Voting starts above $500

More than $500 received in the cycle makes you a voting member. $500 does not qualify — $501 does. Pledged but unpaid amounts don’t count.

Donations are separate

Donations don’t count toward membership, level or voting. Anyone can donate without joining.

Campaign cycle

October 1 – March 31. Gifts received April through September count toward the next cycle.

Installments

Spread your pledge across the six-month cycle. Your level and vote follow what has actually been received.

One member, one vote

Voting members cast a single vote each, whatever they contributed. No proxy votes.

Paying your pledge

Pay in full or in up to 6 installments

A pledge may be split into up to six monthly payments, each rounded up to the nearest $5. All payments must be complete by March 31.

How decisions are made

Clear rules, applied the same way everywhere

  • A voter confirms they have no conflict of interest before voting. A member who declares a conflict may abstain.
  • Quorum: at least 50% of eligible voters (rounded up) must cast a Yes or No vote. Abstentions are recorded but don’t count.
  • If quorum isn’t met the motion fails; a tie fails; otherwise a simple majority of votes cast decides.
  • Charter amendments are shared with all members before any vote.
  • Members sign the charter once at least $250 has been received and the pledge is paid in full.

Where the money goes

USAFI Impact is our fiscal sponsor: it issues every receipt and keeps the official financial records. Payments are processed by WAZIImpact.org — no card details ever touch our site. Grants support partners in USAFI hubs across Sub-Saharan Africa, in four funding portfolios.
