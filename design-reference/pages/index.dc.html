About the Chapter

A giving circle for the diaspora, rooted in Africa.

Impact 100 African Diaspora is a chapter affiliated with Impact 100 Global, built for Africans living in the United States and the allies who stand with them. We pool contributions each campaign cycle and direct them, together, to high-impact organizations in Sub-Saharan Africa doing work their own communities have asked for.

Our purpose

We fund priorities that communities identify for themselves.

Members bring forward organizations they know and trust, learn alongside one another, and make informed grant decisions together.

Giving is only half of it.

The Chapter is also a place for diaspora communities across the US to meet, share knowledge, and build lasting relationships with partners on the continent.

USAFI Impact hosts the Chapter

USAFI Impact hosts the Chapter and holds fiduciary responsibility — handling due diligence, legal compliance, and payment of every approved grant.

How a campaign cycle works

Every member is heard.

Members contributing more than $500 in a cycle cast one equal vote each — no matter how much they gave.

01 · Oct

Pledge

Choose a level from $250 to $1,000 and pay in full or in installments through WAZIImpact.org by March 31.

02

Propose

Members suggest priorities, nominees and local African organizations. Leaders summarize each proposal, its finances and any conflicts.

03

Discuss

Every member gets notice and a fair chance to advise before a vote opens — regardless of contribution level.

04

Vote & grant

Members with more than $500 credited vote once each. USAFI Impact completes due diligence and pays approved grants.
