Section 6 · Quorum and outcomesCirculating

Lower quorum to 40% for decisions that are not grant votes

Proposed by Efua A. (Innovator) · circulated to all members on Jan 12, 2027

Current text

Quorum: at least 50% of eligible voters (rounded up) must cast a Yes or No vote.

Proposed text

Quorum: at least 50% of eligible voters (rounded up) for grant votes, and 40% for other Chapter decisions.

Where it is in the process
  1. Circulating

    Shared with every member on Jan 12, 2027. Minimum circulation: 14 days.

  2. On ballot

    An admin can place it on a ballot after circulation.

  3. Adopted or rejected

    Decided by the quorum and majority rules. If adopted, a new charter version is published.
