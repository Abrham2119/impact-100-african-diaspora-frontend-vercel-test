About the Chapter

A giving circle for the diaspora, rooted in Africa.

Impact 100 African Diaspora is a chapter affiliated with Impact 100 Global, built for Africans living in the United States and the allies who stand with them. We pool contributions each campaign cycle and direct them, together, to high-impact organizations in Sub-Saharan Africa doing work their own communities have asked for.
